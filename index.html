What I measure, and where

The same question — how is this crop coping — asked at three depths. Open a row to see the methods and the hardware behind it.

From above

+20 to +120 m
UAV flight planningMultispectralNDVI / NDRE

Planning multispectral flights over trial plots, then pulling vegetation indices back down to the plot and plant level. The point is to find the rows that are already struggling while the canopy still looks uniform to the eye.

geopandasrasterioxarrayshapelyIDW / kriging

I moved my spatial work out of ArcGIS Pro and into scripted pipelines. Regional and city-scale interpolation with inverse distance weighting and kriging, stacked as data cubes so a result can be rebuilt from a clean checkout instead of remembered as a sequence of clicks.

At the leaf

+0.4 to +2 m
LI-6800Stomatal conductanceChlorophyll fluorescence

Running the LI-6800 portable photosynthesis system on maize and tracking assimilation, conductance and fluorescence through a stress episode. These curves usually move days before anything shows up in a photograph of the plot.

Object detection>95% accuracyNon-destructive

A detection pipeline that counts and measures stomata in microscope imagery without a person at the eyepiece. It agrees with manual counts above 95% of the time and cut dataset turnaround from roughly seven days to one, which is what made high-throughput sampling realistic for a single researcher.

Cross-polarised520–530 nmHyperspectral cubes

Coaxial reflected-light imaging in the 520–530 nm band, cross-polarised to kill the specular sheen off a wet leaf. Paired with chlorophyll fluorescence it maps physiological state across the blade while the leaf is still on the plant.

Hyperspectral cube handling for the DFG-funded MultiStress subproject in Göttingen.

Raspberry Pi 5ESP32-S3RP2040-ZeroI²C / SPITCA9548A16-bit ADC

Mixed sensor stacks on Pi 5, ESP32-S3 and RP2040-Zero: resolving I²C address collisions with multiplexers, adding 16-bit ADCs where the onboard converter is not good enough, and writing everything offline-first so a dropped connection in a field never costs a morning of data.

Why it is built this way

Every design decision on this instrument is a subtraction. A field microscope fails on the things that are fine on a bench: a focus stage that drifts in a breeze, a preload someone has to re-set with cold hands, a clamp that stays on long enough for the leaf to react to being touched. So the spindles are direct-drive, the springs are captive, and the clamp has a hard time limit.

Fifteen seconds, then release.Maize responds to touch. Past roughly fifteen seconds the clamp is no longer observing the leaf, it is changing it — so the measurement window is enforced in firmware, not left to the operator.
Aggregate to the plant before testing.P-values computed over individual leaves of the same plant are pseudoreplication. Means first, statistics second.
Vapour pressure deficit is a variable, not noise.VPD goes into every stress model as an explicit covariate. Stomatal behaviour is not interpretable without it.
If it cannot be re-run, it is not a result.Explicit schema contracts, append-and-flush writes, fixed random seeds. Analysis lives in scripts that run start to finish on a clean machine.
